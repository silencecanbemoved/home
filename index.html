Welcome to Silence Can Be Moved!

SILENCE — noun

We define silence as…

The place young people can find themselves when they feel unable, unwilling, or afraid to speak about what they are going through.

Silence is not always the absence of words. Sometimes it is the weight of protecting your image, hiding your struggles, or pretending that everything is fine. Young people can feel pressure to look strong, because admitting that they are struggling can feel like admitting weakness.

At SCBM, we believe that nobody should have to reach breaking point before they feel able to speak.

Silence is not weakness. Silence is something we can challenge.

MOVED — verb

When we say “moved” we mean…

Taking something that seems impossible to shift and proving that change can begin with something as powerful as a voice.

Silence cannot be physically picked up and moved with our hands. So we move it differently.

We move silence with our words.
We move it by speaking.
We move it by listening.
We move it by creating spaces where people feel heard.

Because if words can move silence, then words can move people, communities, and conversations.

If we cannot move it with our hands, we will move it with our voices.

CAN BE — phrase

By “can be” we mean…

Possibility, not pressure.

We chose “can be” because SCBM is not here to tell people what they should do or what they will do.

We may have a shared mission to help address the mental health crisis, but getting help is personal. Speaking is personal. Change is personal.

“Should be” can sound like an instruction.
“Will be” can sound like a promise made for someone else.

But “can be” leaves the door open.

It says: you can speak when you are ready.
You can ask for help.
You can make a difference.
Things can change.

We don’t force people through the door.

We simply make sure the door is there.
